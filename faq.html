FAQ

Good questions.

Everything you might want to know before your first class.

Classes

Is Centric CrossFit?

No. Every class is the same proven format: strength first, then two intervals that build. It’s coached for form and scaled to you, and the only person you’re competing with is yourself.

What does a class look like?

Each class starts with a functional warm-up, then an 18-minute strength block with a different main lift each day. After that come two cardio-driven intervals (about 12–15 and 9–12 minutes) where you keep moving through full-body exercises. Mobility and core work are built in, and you’re in and out within an hour. See our formula →

I’m out of shape. Can I still join?

Yes. All levels are welcome, from former athletes to people just getting started. It’s the same class with different weights, and your coach scales every move so you can start where you are.

I have an old injury. Is that okay?

Let your coach know before class. Form and safety come first at Centric, and moves can be modified to work around an injury. If you’re recovering from something recent, check with your doctor or physical therapist first. CONFIRM

How big are the classes?

Classes are small groups with a one-on-one feel, so your coach can check your form and adjust moves for you. CONFIRM class size

Do you ever train outside?

On nice days, some workouts include exercises outside. CONFIRM

Getting started

How does the 7-day trial work?

Book online, then come to as many classes as you like for 7 days for $10 CONFIRM. Your coach will give you a quick walkthrough and form check, and at the end of the week you can pick the membership that fits.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable workout clothes, athletic shoes and a water bottle. CONFIRM: anything else, like a towel?

How early should I arrive for my first class?

Plan to arrive about 10 minutes early CONFIRM so your coach can show you around and walk you through the format.

How many times a week should I come?

We recommend 2–3 times a week to start CONFIRM. Your coach can help you build from there.
