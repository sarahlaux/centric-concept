Our story

Why Centric
exists.

Centric started with a passion for movement and the human body, and a simple goal: help people move better and feel stronger, with a challenge that still feels safe and encouraging.

A lot of group classes skip mobility and the movement patterns your body needs, like rotations, holds and functional work. Centric was built to fill that gap: effective, fun and a little competitive, while building strength, healthy joints and heart and lung capacity.
