Welcome to Centric

Small group classes with a one-on-one feel

We believe you'll find your center by being here.

Every class is coached in a small group, so you get the attention of personal training with the energy of people around you. We program functional movement, hold a high standard on form, and train you to move better in daily life, from the core out. Whether it's your first week or your fifth year, the coach knows your name and your numbers.

How a class
breaks down

Every session builds strength first, then raises your heart rate, then finishes for time.

We lift while you're fresh, so your form stays clean and the weight can climb. Cardio comes next to keep your heart rate up while your muscles are already working, and a short finisher pushes the pace. Strength plus cardio is what makes it metabolic, and it's how we fit a full-body workout into under an hour.

  1. 18MinStrength. Controlled lifts and core work at a steady tempo.
  2. 14MinCardio. Jump rope, presses and plyometrics to lift the heart rate.
  3. 11MinFinisher. A capped round for time to leave it all on the floor.
