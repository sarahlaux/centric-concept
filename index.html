About us

Centric is small group classes with a one-on-one feel. Every session pairs strength and cardio, so you're challenged by lifts, tempos and movement while the people around you keep you going. We program functional movement, hold a high standard on form, and train you to move better in daily life, from the core out. We believe you'll find your center by being here.
