01 / About Centric

What is
Centric?

Small group classes with a one-on-one feel.

Every class is full-body: strength, cardio, plyometric and mobility work, with a mix of lifts, intensities and tempos. Pairing strength and cardio in one session makes it metabolic training, and you’re naturally motivated by the people around you. We believe you’ll find your center here.

Each class starts with a functional warm-up and runs 40–50 minutes, so you’re in and out within an hour. We program functional movements and set high standards for quality form. Our goal is to have you moving well in daily life, working from the core out.

03 / How a class works

Every class.
Same format.

Inside Centric

  1. 0118 min

    Strength

    A different main lift every day, plus accessory and mobility work to prevent injury and help you move well.

    Why it works

    You lift while you’re fresh, so your form is sharper and every rep counts. Research on combined training suggests lifting before cardio leads to better strength gains, without costing your endurance.

  2. 0212–15 min

    Interval 1

    Full-body conditioning.

    Why it works

    You keep moving through the exercises for the whole interval, so your heart rate stays up and your heart and lung capacity builds. The workout is planned for you. You choose your weights and when to take a breather.

  3. 039–12 min

    Interval 2

    A harder full-body finish.

    Why it works

    Another nonstop round that builds on the first. Need a break? Take one. Want lighter weights, or a bigger challenge? Go for it. It’s structured for you, and you decide how to attack it.

07 / FAQ

Good
questions.

Is Centric CrossFit?

No. Every class is the same proven format: strength first, then two intervals that build. It’s coached for form and scaled to you.

I’m out of shape. Can I still join?

Yes. All levels are welcome. It’s the same class with different weights, and your coach scales every move so you can start where you are.

How many times a week should I come?

We recommend 2–3 times a week to start CONFIRM. Your coach can help you build from there.
